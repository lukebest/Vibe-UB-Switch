## How to read numbers

- **Area** is Yosys `stat -liberty` **stdcell µm²** at sky130hd tt 1.8 V 25 °C. It is **not** die size, not utilization, not GDS.
- Memories left as `$mem` do **not** appear in that µm². Blocks that still exploded (e.g. `vibe_dll_retry_buf`, `vibe_saf_ing`) flattened storage into stdcells — treat those areas as **upper-bound / flattened**, not SRAM macros.
- **No WNS/TNS.** Timing at FS 1.25 GHz on Sky130 is a known fail risk; we did not invent slack.
